    The secret of the secrets Dan Brown

    Yes! Katherine Solomon has appeared in another Dan Brown novel. In fact, she's a returning character from one of the earlier Robert Langdon adventures.

    1. Katherine Solomon's previous appearance¶

    She appears in two novels:

    Book Year Role
    The Lost Symbol 2009 First appearance
    The Secret of Secrets 2025 Returns as a major character

    In The Lost Symbol, Katherine is the sister of Peter Solomon, Robert Langdon's mentor. She is a scientist specialising in noetic science and human consciousness.

    If you enjoy her character in the current book, I would definitely recommend reading The Lost Symbol. The Recurring Cast

    2. Websites showing locations chapter by chapter¶

    I found something particularly interesting!

    1. Langdon's World – The Secret of Secrets¶

    This is probably exactly what you're looking for.

    It is a visual companion to Dan Brown's novels.

    It includes: - Chapter-by-chapter references. - Photographs of actual locations. - Buildings, monuments and streets mentioned in the story. - Historical and geographical context.

    It starts with the prologue and continues through the chapters to the epilogue.

    2. Hello Prague – Dan Brown's Prague¶

    A guide to the real Prague locations featured in the novel.

    It includes: - Interactive map. - Charles Bridge. - Klementinum. - Prague Castle. - Folimanka Park. - Jewish Quarter.

    Warning: This guide contains plot spoilers.

    3. Official Prague tourist map¶

    The Secret of Secrets – Prague City Tourism

    Prague's official tourist organisation has even produced a special Dan Brown map.

    It identifies 15 real-life locations featured in the novel.

    Available in English, Czech and German.

    My recommendation¶

    Keep Langdon's World open on your tablet or second screen while reading the novel.

    You can follow Robert Langdon's journey, see the actual buildings and streets, and get a much better feeling for Prague.

    It's almost like watching the film while reading the book!
